About this app
PULSE
Minimal habit tracker. One tap a day per habit, and every long-press opens a per-habit consistency graph — a 12-month heatmap plus a 30-day rolling-completion line. Single-file vanilla PWA, single cyan accent, offline-first, installable on phone.
Yes / no habits · tap the circle to mark today done
Tally habits (water, pages, min…) · pick a unit when you add them. Each tap adds one step (e.g. +8 oz, +5 pages). When the target hits, the day marks done.
Tap name or › · open the consistency graph + rename / set target / delete
Shift-click the circle (desktop) · enter a custom amount
